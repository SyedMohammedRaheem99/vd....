FAQ

Common Questions

How long does the consultation take?

Our initial consultation typically takes 1-2 hours. We use this time to understand your vision, assess your space, discuss budget considerations, and provide preliminary recommendations.

How soon can you start my project?

After the consultation and design approval, we can typically begin construction within 2-4 weeks, depending on project scope and current scheduling. Smaller projects may have quicker start times.

Do you work with existing contractors?

Yes! We offer design-only services for clients who prefer to work with their own contractors. We provide complete design documentation and can coordinate with your chosen construction team.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the greater New York metropolitan area and surrounding regions. For larger commercial projects, we are available to travel further. Contact us to discuss your location.

What is included in your warranty?

We offer a comprehensive 10-year structural warranty and 5-year warranty on all installations. Manufacturer warranties apply to materials and fixtures. Our team provides ongoing support after project completion.
