General Questions

Design Vision Constructions & Interiors offers a comprehensive range of services including interior design, full-scale construction, renovation, project management, space planning, material selection, furniture procurement, and custom cabinetry. Whether you're looking to transform a single room or undertake a complete home or commercial renovation, our team has the expertise to bring your vision to life with meticulous attention to detail and premium quality craftsmanship.

With over 15 years of experience in the interior design and construction industry, Design Vision has established itself as a trusted leader in creating exceptional spaces. Our team combines decades of collective expertise with innovative design approaches to deliver projects that exceed expectations. We've successfully completed hundreds of residential and commercial projects, earning recognition for our commitment to quality, innovation, and client satisfaction.

We proudly serve clients throughout the metropolitan area and surrounding regions. Our team is equipped to handle projects both locally and remotely, offering virtual consultations for clients at a distance. For construction projects, we primarily serve the greater metropolitan region within a 50-mile radius, though we're always open to discussing larger projects that may require our expertise beyond this area.

Absolutely! Design Vision has extensive experience working on both residential and commercial projects. Our residential services include complete home renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels, basement finishing, and custom new construction. For commercial clients, we offer office design and buildouts, retail space transformations, restaurant and hospitality design, and professional workspace optimization. Each project, regardless of scale, receives our full attention to detail and commitment to excellence.

At Design Vision, we distinguish ourselves through our comprehensive approach that combines exceptional design with expert construction capabilities. Unlike firms that only provide design services, we offer complete solutions from concept to completion, eliminating the communication gaps that often occur between designers and contractors. Our commitment to transparency, quality craftsmanship, and personalized service ensures that every project reflects our clients' unique vision while meeting the highest standards of excellence.

Services & Process

Our design process begins with an in-depth consultation where we listen to your needs, preferences, and vision. We then conduct a thorough space analysis and develop conceptual designs, including mood boards, space plans, and material palettes. Upon approval, we create detailed construction drawings and 3D renderings to help you visualize the final result. Throughout the construction phase, we manage every detail, ensuring communication and quality control. Our collaborative approach ensures your vision is realized while our expertise guides decisions for optimal results.

Yes, we offer advanced 3D rendering and visualization services as part of our design package. These photorealistic renderings allow you to see your space before construction begins, helping you make informed decisions about materials, colors, furniture placement, and design details. We believe this transparency is essential for ensuring client satisfaction and minimizing changes during construction. Additional virtual reality experiences are available for larger projects upon request.

We happily collaborate with architects, builders, and other professionals. Many of our projects involve coordinated efforts where we focus on interior design while working alongside other trusted experts. Our team is experienced in seamless integration with existing project teams, and we maintain clear communication throughout to ensure all aspects of the project work harmoniously together.

Yes, we offer comprehensive project management services for all our projects. From initial concept through final installation, our experienced project managers oversee every detail, coordinate with contractors and suppliers, manage timelines and budgets, and provide regular updates. This full-service approach ensures your project stays on track and meets our high standards of quality and client satisfaction.

Our design team excels in a diverse range of interior styles, from contemporary minimalism and modern luxury to classic traditional and transitional designs. We believe in creating spaces that reflect each client's unique personality and lifestyle. Whether you prefer clean lines and neutral palettes or rich textures and bold accents, we'll create a cohesive design that perfectly suits your taste and functional needs.

Yes, sustainability is an important aspect of our design philosophy. We offer eco-friendly material options, energy-efficient lighting solutions, and sustainable furniture choices. Our team can help you achieve LEED certification or other environmental standards if desired. We work with suppliers who prioritize sustainable sourcing and manufacturing practices, ensuring your project minimizes environmental impact without compromising on quality or aesthetics.

Process & Timeline

Project timelines vary based on scope, size, and complexity. A single room renovation typically takes 4-8 weeks, while whole-home projects may require 3-6 months or more. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed timeline specific to your project. We pride ourselves on efficient project management while never compromising on quality, and we keep clients informed throughout every phase of the process.

Our initial consultation is a comprehensive session where we discuss your vision, needs, and goals for the project. We'll assess your space, explore design possibilities, and answer any questions you may have. We'll also explain our process, services, and pricing structure. This 60-90 minute meeting helps us understand your style preferences and ensures we're the right fit for your project. Consultations can be conducted in-person or virtually for your convenience.

We understand that evolving needs and preferences are natural during a project. Our process includes structured change order procedures with clear documentation and pricing updates. We communicate proactively about any impacts to timeline or budget, ensuring you're always informed. Our goal is flexibility within reason while maintaining project momentum and quality standards.

During construction, our team manages all aspects of the build, from demolition to final installation. We maintain a clean and safe work environment, provide regular progress updates, and coordinate with subcontractors and suppliers. We conduct weekly site meetings to discuss progress, address concerns, and ensure the project stays on schedule. Our project managers serve as your primary point of contact throughout this phase.

It depends on the scope of work and which areas of your home are being renovated. For kitchen or bathroom remodels, many clients choose to remain in the home with some adjustments to their daily routine. For whole-home renovations or projects involving extensive dust and noise, temporary relocation may be more comfortable. We always discuss living arrangements during the planning phase and work to minimize disruption regardless of your choice.

Pricing & Budget

Our pricing varies based on project scope and service level. We offer design-only services starting at project-based rates, as well as full-service design-build solutions. After our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed proposal outlining costs for your specific project. We work with various budgets and are happy to discuss options that align with your financial plans while delivering exceptional quality.

Yes, we offer complimentary initial consultations where we discuss your project and provide general guidance on potential investment levels. Detailed project estimates are provided after our thorough design development phase, ensuring accuracy and transparency. We believe in honest, upfront pricing with no hidden surprises.

We accept various payment methods including credit cards, bank transfers, and checks. For larger projects, we typically structure payments in phases aligned with project milestones. We also work with select financing partners for clients who prefer payment plans. Our team will provide clear payment schedules and terms in your project proposal.

Design fees vary based on project complexity and service requirements. For design-only engagements, we typically charge hourly rates or flat project fees. For design-build projects where we handle both design and construction, the design fee is often incorporated into the overall project cost. During your consultation, we'll clearly outline all fees and how they apply to your specific project.

Our comprehensive quotes include all design services, construction labor, materials, project management, permits, and cleanup. We provide detailed breakdowns so you understand exactly what you're paying for. Any items or services not included in the original quote will be clearly identified before work begins. Our commitment to transparency means no unexpected costs or hidden fees.

Warranty & Support

We stand behind our workmanship with a comprehensive 10-year structural warranty and 5-year warranty on installations. Manufacturer warranties apply to all materials used, and we'll help you navigate any warranty claims if needed. Our commitment to quality means we're here to support you long after your project is complete.

Yes, our team is available for ongoing support and maintenance after project completion. We also offer styling services to help you accessorize and personalize your completed space. Whether you need guidance on furniture selection, color updates, or minor repairs, we're just a phone call away and committed to ensuring your complete satisfaction.

While we strive for perfection in every project, we understand that issues can sometimes arise. Our warranty coverage ensures that any defects in workmanship will be addressed promptly at no additional cost. Simply contact our office, and we'll schedule a time to assess and repair any covered issues. Our goal is to maintain long-term relationships with our clients and ensure your complete peace of mind.
