FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and what to expect when working with us.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A single room renovation typically takes 4-8 weeks, while whole-home projects may span 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during the consultation phase and keep you updated throughout the process.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer transparent, detailed pricing with no hidden costs. Our quotes include all materials, labor, and project management fees. We work within various budgets and can suggest alternatives if needed. A detailed breakdown is provided before any work begins.

Do you provide free consultations?

Yes, we offer a complimentary initial consultation to discuss your project, assess your needs, and provide preliminary recommendations. This allows us to understand your vision and for you to experience our approach before committing to the project.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the greater metropolitan area and surrounding suburbs. For larger commercial projects, we are available to travel further. Please contact us to discuss your location and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.

What warranties do you offer?

We stand behind our workmanship with a comprehensive 10-year structural warranty and 5-year warranty on installations. Additionally, manufacturer warranties apply to all materials used. Our team is available for ongoing support and maintenance after project completion.
